Live intelligence

Answers from what's true right now.

Most knowledge tools answer from a snapshot — what was true the last time they crawled. DocBrain reaches into your live systems at the moment you ask, then combines that with your indexed knowledge into one cited answer.

Read-only by design

DocBrain can read your tools, never write to them. Any tool that could change a system is dropped at discovery. Enforced, not promised.

Each user, their own permissions

Connected per-user over OAuth. Your answer is scoped to what you can see in the source. Tokens refresh on their own, and nobody sees data they shouldn't.

Any MCP-enabled system

Jira, Confluence and Slack out of the box, plus anything that speaks the Model Context Protocol. Add a tool, and DocBrain starts answering from it.

Indexed history + live state, one answer

Documentation autopilot & generate

The memory writes the documents.

Autopilot clusters the questions your organization couldn't answer, detects the gaps behind them, and drafts what's missing. docbrain generate turns that memory into a runbook, postmortem or onboarding guide on demand — with per-claim provenance.

Documentation Autopilot

Clusters unanswered questions, detects the gaps behind them, and auto-drafts the missing documentation — routed to human review. Nothing publishes without oversight.

Grounded, not generic

A general AI model writes fluent, generic prose. generate writes what is true for you — grounded in your incidents, tickets, decisions and past work — or tells you honestly what it can't answer.

Your agent fixes it. DocBrain remembers it.

Engineers now solve their hardest problems inside agent sessions — and that knowledge dies when the terminal closes. DocBrain's MCP tools give your agent a write path: it checks whether the org already knows the fix, drafts a capture, and asks you before filing it.

Security & enterprise readiness

Built to pass your security review.

DocBrain is designed for the teams whose job is to say no. It runs entirely inside your infrastructure, reads your systems without ever writing to them, and shows its work on every answer.

Self-hosted, zero egress

Read-only by design

Straight answers

What we haven't proven yet.

A product built on refusing to overclaim should not overclaim about itself.

  • The server is closed source. The client that runs inside your network is MIT-licensed and auditable; the server isn't published yet. We targeted the first half of 2026, missed it, and won't name a new date until we're certain of it. If that's a dealbreaker, it's a rational position and we respect it.
  • We publish no measured accuracy benchmark. Grounding is measured internally, but no number goes on this page until it's measured against real customer data — and when it does, it arrives with its methodology.
  • Self-hosting isn't unique to us. Open-source alternatives exist and several are very good at retrieval. What they don't do is capture knowledge that was never written down. Evaluate us on that.
  • Our evidence base is strongest for software teams. The mechanism generalises across sectors; the published statistics haven't been measured everywhere, and we won't imply otherwise.
